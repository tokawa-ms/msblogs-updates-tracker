<details><summary>English summary</summary>

Microsoft and NVIDIA published Risk Management for Open-Weight Models in Enterprise, a joint paper to help enterprises assess and manage risks from open-weight models. It argues that risk depends on the model, use case, deployment, and safeguards—not weight openness alone—and presents a shared taxonomy and assessment approach grounded in ISO and NIST frameworks. The first paper focuses mainly on the model layer, with future work intended to cover runtime and application layers.

**Why it matters:** For engineering, security, legal, safety, and business teams, the paper provides a shared taxonomy and guidance for connecting deployment-specific risks to evaluations, mitigations, and accountable owners. Because model-only assessment or weight openness does not establish system-wide safety, teams need to consider the actual permissions, data, tools, operations, and responsibility for safeguards.

**Key points**
- The initial taxonomy covers five areas: cybersecurity, data protection, content, human-AI interaction, and bias. It is not a deployment checklist; enterprises are expected to adapt it to their systems and use cases and connect risks to evaluations, mitigations, and accountable owners.
